Engineering standards.
Verifiable delivery.

At OSEF, getting software to run is only the start. We also aim to demonstrate repeatable deployment, secure configuration, reliable operations, maintenance and skills transfer.

Proposed standard v0.1, piloted in two repositories. Not yet formally adopted organization-wide; no production certification or architectural compliance is implied.

THE TWELVE-FACTOR APP

Twelve factors. Verifiable evidence.

OSEF's review criteria derive from the Twelve-Factor App method and are adapted for enterprise integration and operations.

01 / 12

Codebase

Versioned code, manifests and infrastructure

02 / 12

Dependencies

Declared dependencies and identified versions

03 / 12

Config

Configuration separated from code, with protected secrets

04 / 12

Backing services

External dependencies and storage explicitly documented

05 / 12

Build, release, run

Separate build, release and run stages

06 / 12

Processes

Document persistent state; stateless processes where appropriate

07 / 12

Port binding

Explicit and controlled interfaces and ports

08 / 12

Concurrency

Measured concurrency or explicitly stated limits

09 / 12

Disposability

Verified shutdown, restart and recovery

10 / 12

Dev/prod parity

Documented environment differences

11 / 12

Logs

Log collection and sensitive-data protection

12 / 12

Admin processes

Reproducible, traceable administration

ENGINEER CONTRIBUTION PATH

How engineers apply OSEF-ENG-012

1Review

Read the reference standard and identify applicable factors.

2Demonstrate

Record actual tests, evidence, limitations and justified exceptions in the matrix.

3Get reviewed

Submit a Pull Request for CI structure checks and peer engineering review.

CI currently checks documentation structure, not real Twelve-Factor compliance. Mandatory branch protection is not yet enabled.

APPLIED WITH JUDGMENT

One reference, adapted to different systems

Applications & APIs

Apply the original Twelve-Factor practices where appropriate and validate actual behavior.

Integrated platforms

Nextcloud, BlueMind, Tazama and databases may be stateful. Document storage, backup, restore, upgrades and rollback instead of claiming statelessness.

Engineering labs

Keep the technical proof, business explanation and meetup or knowledge-sharing deliverables distinct and reproducible.

OUR PURPOSE

Vision, mission & strategic goals

Make Open Source an African industrial engineering capability, not merely an alternative software choice.

OUR VISION

World-class enterprise Open Source engineering rooted in Africa.

Build an Africa-born reference organization that can design, integrate and operate open technologies at African and international scale.

OUR MISSION

Turn open technologies into reliable enterprise solutions.

Deliver secure, documented and operable Open Source solutions through a pan-African network of skilled engineers and contributors.

03Business development

Develop sustainable advisory, integration and operations services.

04Skills

Strengthen specializations and professional development.

05Upstream contributions

Contribute to international Open Source projects and communities.

06Technology autonomy

Improve control of architectures, infrastructure, data and expertise.

07Governance and sustainability
